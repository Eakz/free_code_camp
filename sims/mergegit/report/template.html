How these numbers were made

  • Feral: Patchwerk (raid, 6,000–10,000 iterations, ±0.06–0.08%) and DungeonSlice (M+, 4,000–6,000 iterations, ±0.21–0.25%). Bear: Patchwerk, one tank per run, 10,000 iterations (DPS ±0.09%, damage taken ±0.35%).
  • Single-swap gains are shown on each changed item; set totals come from simming the whole set together.
  • Kith'ix (The Unbinding of Kith'ix, 12.1.5) items: Mythic drops at 324 and upgrades to 334. Lightbearer's Shield Fragment (its tank trinket) and Font of Venomous Rage both made the bear take more damage, so they are left out. Gaze of the Coiled Watcher and Chestwrap of Palpable Terror both use the one non-tier slot; Chestwrap won.
  • Crests: the bear build needs 100 Hero crests (Cinch 305→321), the cat build 40 (Amani ring 315→321). You have 154. The projected tier gauntlets need 80 more (308→321).
  • Projected BiS items come from murlok.io's top-50 Feral M+ usage (read through web search; the site could not be opened here). Raid items are simmed at Myth 6/6 (334). Raid rings are simmed without a gem because their socket is not confirmed.
  • Icons are generic WoW slot, gem and enchant icons, not the exact item icons: the item-to-icon data lives on Wowhead and wago, which are blocked from the sim machine.
  • Costs: two-handers need 4 Sparks of Tides, armour 2, each 331 craft 80 Myth Mistcrests (you have 102). Hero upgrades cost 20 crests per step.
  • Sources: murlok Feral M+, Styka loot database, Method Guardian gearing.
