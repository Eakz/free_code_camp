How this was made

  • SimC midnight 710c240 (2026-10-28), game data 12.1.5.70575 = your client. Exports: Guardian 2026-10-29 02:00 and Balance 02:03. Items are simmed at their upgrade cap (Hero 321, Myth 334, crafted 331; your Venomstoned 341 pieces as worn). Crafting new items and new Venomstones were out of scope: "what I have".
  • Bear: one run per variant, 3,000 iterations to screen 88 single changes, 8,000 for the final sets, five attacking heroic mobs as the pull model. Balance: SimC profilesets, 6,000 / 3,500 iterations to screen 103 variants and 10,000 / 6,000 for the finals. All runs include a control copy that came back at 0.00%.
  • The heroic mob strength is a stand-in for a hard key. Damage taken does not count max health, which is why Swarmcaller looks worse for the bear than it is.
  • Your gloves carry the enchant "Ancient Hash'ey Battle Art: Eye of the Lynx". SimC does not model it, so its value is not in these numbers.
  • Embellishment cap (2) holds in every set. Whether two Arcanoweave pieces are allowed was confirmed by you.
  • Raw inputs and outputs: sims/mergegit/opt_1029 on branch claude/epic-cori-kqdhlt.
