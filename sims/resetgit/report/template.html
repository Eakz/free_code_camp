How these numbers were made

  • Raid: Patchwerk, 20,000 iterations, error ±0.04%. M+: DungeonSlice, 10,000 iterations, error ±0.23%. Treat M+ differences under 0.5% as ties. A control copy of your own gear came back at +0.06% / +0.03%.
  • Caps: Champion 308, Hero 321, Myth 334, crafted 331. Dungeons stop at 321. Kith'ix and Venomous Abyss items are simmed at Myth 6/6 (334).
  • Your hands sit at Myth 2/6 (321). They are simmed at 334, which costs 4 upgrades × 20 = 80 Myth crests; you have 25. Hero crests: 105, enough for five 321 upgrade steps.
  • Catalyst charges could not be read from the export. Check in game before planning on tier legs; without them use the Murder Row legs.
  • Boss sources disagree in places. *Boots of the Reckless Wayfarer: most guides say The Lost Explorers, Wowhead says Mor'zahi. Crown of the Eternal Fang: Nek'zali or Entombed Sentinels. Cuisses of the Uncoiled Union: most guides say The Coiled Altar.
  • Icons are generic WoW slot icons, not exact item icons (the icon data lives on sites blocked from the sim machine).
  • Sources: WowVendor Venomous Abyss loot, WowCarry loot table, Gearpath Coiled Altar, Warcraft Wiki: Vile Alchemist's Band, Warcraft Wiki: Amani Summoning Shawl, Method Kith'ix items, Method bonus rolls, Styka loot database.
