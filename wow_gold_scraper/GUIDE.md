# WoW Retail · Midnight Season 2 · EU · solo — top 10 gold methods

Compiled 2026-10-07 from pages fetched by `wowgold.py` (raw copies in `out/pages/`). Season 2 launched 18 Aug 2026 (patch 12.1 on 11 Aug), so we are ~7 weeks in: the post-patch "lull" (Lazy Goldmaker, 2026-07-23: lulls start 3-5 weeks after a patch; gear/enchant demand falls, consumables sell at lower margins, cosmetic long-term markets are the recommended place to spread out). Patch 12.1.5 is expected around early November (Lazy Goldmaker, 2026-09-15: "5-6 weeks").

**Confidence labels:** A = two or more independent sources, B = one dated primary-style source, C = gold-seller/booster site only (use for places/items, never for its gold-per-hour). **Live EU prices are NOT yet included** (needs Blizzard API keys); every gold figure below is a source's claim, not a measurement.

Sources: IV = Icy Veins (updated 11 Aug 2026) · MG = Method.gg · LG = The Lazy Goldmaker (dated posts) · MM = mmogah (gold seller) · WV = WoWVendor (gold seller).

## 1. Alchemy + 2nd profession on Concentration (alt-based) — A
- **What:** Potion of Recklessness / Light's Potential (Wowhead class guides split ~evenly between them, LG 2026-08-18). Flasks rank Blood Knights > Magisters > Shattered Sun. Potions are worth ~3-5 g per Concentration; Jewelcrafting/Enchanting ~1-2 g (LG 2026-09-08). IV tier list: Alchemy potions S-tier, flasks A-tier (weaker).
- **How:** Concentration regenerates 10/hour. Max multicraft first, then resourcefulness; get a rare/epic tool with a multicraft enchant (LG 06-23, 06-16). Light's Potential needs no recipe purchase; Potion of Recklessness is bought with Voidlight Marl. Open the profession window, run CraftSim's recipe scan, craft the highest Concentration-value recipe, buy mats on the AH, post immediately (LG 08-30: "every auction sells instantly").
- **Money:** LG's own plan implies ~720 g/day per alt (21,600 g per 3 days across 10 crafters, 2026-09-15). That is **per character**, so a solo player with 1-3 alts earns little from this alone; it scales with alts, not hours.
- **Epic tools:** need Fused Vitality (20 per piece) from Dundun's Abundance event (see #7).

## 2. Crafting orders for crafted (spark) gear — B
- **What:** weapons, bracers, rings, cloak with the Arcanoweave Lining embellishment (top choice for almost every spec, LG 08-18); chest enchant Mark of the Worldsoul (38/40 specs).
- **How:** you earn the customer's tip; ask 3-4 crafters advertising in trade chat what they charge; advertise with the Autoflood addon (`/floodmsg`, `/floodrate 180`, `/floodchan 2`) (LG 08-12). IV: best order gold is mid-to-late week and just before/after popular raid hours.
- **Caveat:** very patch-driven; the biggest day was when the new spark opened (18 Aug). Expect the next burst with 12.1.5.

## 3. Housing decor from Lumber — B
- **Where/what:** Thalassian Lumber (BoP, so only farmers can make decor) — LG tested Harandar: 37 lumber in 10 min on a Death Knight without flight form (~200+/h). Decor needs 6-30 lumber each; profit 300-500 g per lumber; sale rate per item is low, so post many items (LG 2026-07-10).
- **How:** farm on one character (a Druid is faster), craft across professions via crafting orders to yourself, take recipes with the best profit per lumber first. 12.1 added decor recipes from fishing (LG 08-07).
- **Claim:** "≥60k g/h" is LG's low estimate, unverified.

## 4. Corin's Crossing / The Cauldron, Eastern Plaguelands (old world) — A (prices C)
- **What:** Dark Runes (~160 g avg, range 150-220) and Essence of Undeath (~100 g on EU), per MM (undated "at time of writing").
- **Solo version:** the Cauldron area next to Corin's Crossing has humanoids that drop Dark Runes, fixed (non-hyper) respawn, so it is semi-AFK: clear, wait, repeat (MM). Group of 5: ~200 runes + ~100 essence per hour (MM claims ~50k g/h). LG uses the same farm as an example: ~10k g per 20 minutes (2026-09-15).
- **Gap:** no map coordinates in the sources; use the in-game map. Verify rune/essence prices before spending time.

## 5. Cooking mass-crafting (AFK) — A
- **How:** buy mats when cheap, craft hundreds, post ~12 h durations just before raid reset day; use cancel-scanning in TSM (LG 09-22). Royal Roast: range 30-40 g, break-even ~32 g, selling at 38 ≈ 20% margin; region volume ~130,000/day. Puffer Plate (short Coiled Isle questline): 40%+ margin but only ~2,700/day sold.
- **Setup:** no knowledge points; Pandaren cook (+5 skill); get Flora Frenzy (Harandar NW quest "Late Bloomers" from Ney'leia); a multicraft enchant on the tool beats upgrading to epic (LG 07-02). Margins are thin: price-check first.

## 6. Prey / Ral'kala feeder farm (Coiled Isle) — A (indirect gold)
- **Where:** Magister Astalor Bloodsworn, Murder Row, Silvermoon → portal to the Coiled Isle; daily quest at 57.1, 33.3 gives 100 Ossified Relics; 100 relics at a Haunted Brazier summons Ral'kala, killable many times a day (IV 2026-08-08).
- **What:** Voidlight Marl (needed for the Potion of Recklessness recipe and Enchanting renown recipes), Remnants of Anguish, crests, pet/toy/mount. Hunts take 7-10 min (MG).
- **Note:** this makes gold through #1, not by selling drops directly.

## 7. Abundance event (Dundun) → epic profession tools — A (enabler)
- 3-minute event in a cave in each zone, rotating every 8 h; 8 Shards of Dundun per week, and since 12.0.7 you can spend all 8 on one run for 8x rewards; Fused Vitality costs 800 Unalloyed Abundance (900 per run). Shards come from zone treasures, mostly Zul'Aman (IV events guide; LG 06-16, 08-07). Epic tools raise multicraft yield ~3.8% and lift every craft in #1-#5.

## 8. Skinning Renowned Beasts — C
- **Where:** (WV waypoints) Gloomclaw, Eversong `/way #2395 41.95 79.70`; Silverscale, Zul'Aman `/way #2437 47.55 53.65`; Lumenfin, Harandar `/way #2413 66.63 47.83`; Umbrafang, Voidstorm `/way #2405 54.15 65.27`; Netherscythe, Voidstorm `/way #2405 43.13 82.81`.
- **What:** Majestic Claw / Fin / Hide; talents Talented Tracker and Majestic Materials. IV rates Skinning B-tier with a material glut. Waypoints are unverified; check prices first.

## 9. Herb + ore gathering (feeder, not a top earner) — A
- MG routes: Mote of Light (Eversong, along rivers), Wild Magic (Zul'Aman, zone edges), Primal Energy (Harandar, most herbs), Pure Void (Voidstorm, ledges + aggressive mobs, needs most of the campaign). Nocturnal Lotus drops rarely from any herb node; take the Midnight Overload specialization.
- IV puts Mining/Herbalism/Skinning in **B-tier** ("massive glut", 11 Aug 2026) and LG says crafting beats gathering per hour. Do it to feed your own crafting or when the Rank 2 herb price is high.

## 10. Multi-realm battle-pet flipping (lull market) — B
- LG's routine: five realms, post 48 h, repost every 3-5 days, ~100 pets listed per realm; he projects ~15,000 g/day (2026-08-30, 09-15). Needs weeks to build stock and a pet-cage addon; best in the lull when cosmetics outsell gear.

## Dropped from the first (Haiku) pass
- "Dual gathering 100-150k g/h = rank 1": contradicted by IV (B-tier, glut) and LG.
- Ringing Deeps / Hallowfall routes, Darkflame Cleft cloth farm: War Within content.
- "Petrified Root sells for ~1,400 g from Delves": not supported; IV/LG describe it as an enchanting/vantus material whose price spikes.
- Delves/Prey as gold: they drop gear and crests; Delve loot stops improving at Tier 8 (IV).
- Booster-site gold-per-hour tiers (200k+/h): marketing, not evidence.
- "Tuesday raid reset": that is the US; EU reset is Wednesday.
