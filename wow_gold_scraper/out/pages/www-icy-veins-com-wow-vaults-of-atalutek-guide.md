# Vaults of Atal'Utek Zone Guide
Support Our Writers
The Vaults of Atal'Utek are a new zone in the Midnight expansion, serving as a complimentary to 
the Coiled Isle in Patch 12.1 and providing challenging open world group content. Learn how to enter 
the Vaults, their activities and rewards!
## Entering the Vaults of Atal'Utek
The Vaults of Atal'Utek are first explored during the main story questline for Patch 12.1. We enter 
the area for the first time in the quest The Vaults of Atal'Utek: Altar of Fangs . As we delve deeper into the 
Vaults, there any many quests along our journey there, starting with Vaults of Atal'Utek: Certain Doom .
All the available activities within the Vaults of Atal'Utek are unlocked as you progress through the Vaults of Atal'Utek: Certain Doom questline.
### Vaults of Atal'Utek Entrance
The Vaults are accessible from three entrances located around the Coiled Isle :
- The Gate of the Serpent's Eye is the northern entrance, located in the Venom's Rise area of the
  Coiled Isle.
- The Gate of the Western Fang is the western entrance, located by the Forum area of the Coiled 
  Isle.
- The Gate of the Eastern Fang is the eastern entrance, located just past the broken bridge 
  southwest of Tokka's Landing.
## Activities in the Vaults of Atal'Utek
The Vaults of Atal'Utek have three "tiers" of activities that feed into each other. Completing 
these activities in rewards you with Corrosive Coin s, reputation with the Zul'Jarra's Forces Renown , and many other rewards.
Here's how the content cadence works for the Vaults:
- Temple Patrols spawn every 10 minutes and are doable solo.
- Temple Strikes spawn once a number of Temple Patrols are done, and usually require 3-5 
 people.
- Temple Incursions spawn once two Temple Strikes are done, or once every hour, whichever 
 happens first. Incursions require 10-20 people, and spawn a boss at the end called an 'Ancient Foe'.
## Vaults of Atal'Utek Activities
Temple Patrols are the lowest tier of activities in the Vaults of Atal'Utek. They are
  scattered across the Vaults, and you're likely stumbling across them by simply venturing
  around the temple. Patrols are marked on your map via a cogwheel vignette, and a new set
  of patrols spawn every 10 minutes.
Temple Patrols can be completed solo with ease.
Temple Strikes are the second tier of activities in the Vaults of Atal'Utek. These spawn upon 
  completing a certain number of Temple Patrols, and trigger a small zone event.
Although Temple Strikes can be completed solo, 3-5 players is the recommended amount for one.
Temple Incursions are the highest tier of activities in the Vaults of Atal'Utek. Triggered after
  completing two Temple Strikes, these are massive zone-wide events. Every Temple Incursion ends
  with a final boss fight.
It is recommended to bring 15-20 players for a Temple Incursion.
## The Altar of Corrosion
As you engage with these activities, you'll collect Corrosive Coin . Their purpose
is to feed the Altar of Corrosion, a new power system that increases your power while you're
within the Vaults of Atal'Utek.
You can start working towards unlocking the Altar of Corrosion with the quest Vaults of Atal'Utek: Certain Doom ,
which serves as an introduction to the Vaults themselves. Once the Altar of Corrosion is unlocked, 
you can talk with Er'inye near the Altar to start corroding your spirit. This 
uses Corrosive Coin s to earn talent points for the Altar of Corrosion, with each point
costing more coins than the previous one.
Every time you corrode your spirit with Erin'ye, you will gain one point of Spirit Corrosion,
which is used to add points into the Altar of Corrosion. Certain talents require completion of
quests before you can allocate them, which are listed in the table below.

## The Corrosive Codex
Another feature from the Vaults of Atal'Utek is the Corrosive Codex. The Codex is a series of 
poison-related powers that are unlocked with Corrosive Soul , a Warband item obtained from 
completing activities in the Coiled Isle, Vaults of Atal'Utek, and Midnight Season 2.
In total there are twelve Corrosive Codex abilities you can obtain, and you are able to use up to two 
at a time. Each ability requires a total of 8 Corrosive Soul to unlock, so to gain access 
to all 12 abilities, you will need 96 Corrosive Souls.
Trait | Description
Ophidian Maw | Damaging and enemy has a chance to call a giant snake that erupts from below, gnashing at the 
	target's location and dealing Nature damage split between nearby enemies. Any Poisoned non-boss 
	creature below 20% is executed.
Insidious Venom | Chance during combat to Poison yourself, lowering all your Secondary and Tertiary stats 
	while greatly increasing your Primary stat for 12 seconds.
Vipering Grasp | Your harmful spells and abilities have a chance to pull up to 2 nearby enemies to your 
	current target's location, dealing Nature damage to affected targets. Up to 3 nearby Poisoned 
	enemies are also pulled in.
Virulent Mucus | Upon entering combat, veil yourself in 5 stacks of Virulent Mucus. Each stack negates an 
	instance of incoming damage that would exceed 5% of your maximum health. Dealing damage to a
	poisoned enemy has a very low chance of granting an additional stack of Virulent Mucus.
Mephitic Cloud | Chance during combat to apply Mephitic Cloud to nearby party members and enemies for 10 seconds.
	Affected allies' spells and abilities Poison their target, dealing Nature damage over 5 seconds.
	Enemy spells and abilities additionally heal their target.
Accursed Poison | Chance during combat to inflict Accursed Poison to the strongest enemy, dealing Nature damage 
	every second for 1 minute. The next time the afflicted target would cast an interruptible spell 
	or ability, the Accursed Poison triggers, interrupting the ability and removing itself.
Gorgoneion Gaze | Upon dropping below 50% health. petrify all enemies within 10 yards for 10 seconds. Petrified
	enemies are stunned and invulnerable. When they break free, enemies become Poisoned, taking 
	Nature damage every second and slowed by 50% for 1 seconds. 1 minute cooldown.
Plague of Corrosion | Chance during combat to apply a stacking Poison to all nearby enemies, dealing Nature damage 
	every 2 seconds for 1 minute. On death, the Poison and its remaining duration are transferred to 
	a new target.
Lithic Plumage | Taking damage has a chance to fortify you, reducing incoming damage by 75% up to a cap. The 
	damage cap is increased for each nearby Poisoned enemy.
Miasma Geyser | Chance during combat to stir up a Miasma Geyser under a nearby enemy. It erupts after 10 seconds,
	dealing Nature damage split between enemies while also healing allies inside. Poisoned units take
	increased damage and healing.
Ouroboric Cycle | Chance during combat to begin an Ouroboric Cycle, increasing all Tertiary stats for 5 seconds, 
	then increasing all Secondary stats for 5 seconds, then increasing your Primary stat for 5 
	seconds. In this state, slaying an enemy causes an additional Cycle to occur after the last. 
	While you are poisoned, each Cycle's duration is extended by one second.
Ula'tek's Gift | Your harmful spells and abilities have a hance to afflict your target with 5 stacks of 
	Ula'Tek's Gift, dealing Nature damage per application every 5 seconds for 1 minute. While you 
	are poisoned, apply 5 additional stacks. Upon applying 20 stacks to a target, the gift corrodes, 
	dealing the remainder of its damage immediately and removing itself.

## Vaults of Atal'Utek Achievements
The Vaults of Atal'Utek have their own meta achievement. Assault the Vault requires you to
participate in several activities in the area, and rewards you with the Venomous Coiler mount.
Achievement | Requirement
Roll the Patrol | Complete every Temple Patrol event in Atal'Utek. Patrols rotate every 10 minutes.
Spike the Strike | Complete every Temple Strike event in Atal'Utek.
Submerge the Incursion | Complete every Temple Incursion event in Atal'Utek. Temple Incursions trigger after completing
	two Temple Strikes.
Oppose the Foes | Defeat all three Ancient Foes in Atal'Utek. An Ancient Foe is the end boss of a Temple Incursion
	event.
A Lone Wanderer | Collect enough Soul Globes in Atal'Utek. These are fairly large and hard to miss, and spawn
	during the Purifying Earth and Sky Temple Strike.
Dance While Everyone Watches | Perform a /dance while defending each stone statue in the Cache of the Three Temple 
	Incursion. This can be completed over multiple incursions.
Ritual Behavior | Collect and deliver all offerings available at the Summoning Ritual Temple Incursion.
	This can be completed over multiple incursions.
Soft Underbelly | Defeat all named Elite mobs in the Underbelly area in Atal'Utek. The Underbelly access opens
	up during the The Underbelly Temple Strike. This will require multiple clears of the 
	strike to complete.
The Honored Dead | Locate all Funerary Inscriptions scattered throughout Atal'Utek. These are plaques at walls 
	and fairly easy to see.
Fully Corroded Title Reward: Playername the Snake | Unlock all traits in the Altar of Corrosion.
