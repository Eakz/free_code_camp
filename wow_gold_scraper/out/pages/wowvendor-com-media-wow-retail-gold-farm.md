### Crafting professions
Crafting professions usually take longer to become profitable because you need Profession Knowledge, recipes, materials, and specialization progress.
However, a well-developed crafter gets access to many more ways of making gold, including consumables , crafted gear , collectibles , and Crafting Orders .
Some of the best crafting professions for solo gold farming are:
- Alchemy — potions, flasks, and other consumables remain popular among raiders and M+ players.
- Enchanting — players constantly need enhancements for newly obtained or crafted gear.
- Jewelcrafting — gems, accessories, and collectibles can remain profitable throughout an entire season.
- Inscription — provides access to glyphs and other useful crafted goods.
- Engineering — offers several valuable mounts, toys, and unusual collectibles.
Crafting professions require more preparation than gathering, but they can become a reliable long-term source of income.

Note
Farming transmog and collectibles does not guarantee a quick sale. An item may be worth hundreds of thousands or even millions of gold but remain on the Auction House for weeks or months before somebody buys it.
### Auction House flipping
Auction House flipping is one of the most efficient gold-making methods , but it also requires much more market knowledge. The basic idea is simple: buy an item when it is listed too cheaply and sell it later for a higher price. For example, a player may place materials significantly below their normal market value because they want a quick sale. A knowledgeable trader can buy those materials and relist them closer to their normal price.
However, successful flipping requires understanding:
- normal item prices;
- sale rates;
- market demand;
- material costs;
- seasonal demand;
- competition;
- how quickly an item usually sells.
This is why addons such as TradeSkillMaster are particularly useful for Auction House traders. Do not invest all your gold into a single item simply because its current price looks low. Prices can change quickly, and some expensive items have very low demand. Starting with small investments is much safer until you understand how your realm and regional markets behave.
