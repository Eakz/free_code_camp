# Best Consumables in WoW Midnight Patch 12.1: Flasks, Food, Runes & Potions
Consumables remain one of the easiest “free” power spikes in Midnight. Gear upgrades still take time, lockouts, and luck. A quick trip to the Auction House (or a short crafting session) immediately raises your baseline damage, healing, and survivability.
Patch 12.1 (Curse of Ula’tek / Coiled Isle content) added meaningful upgrades—especially to health potions and food—while leaving the core flask and rune systems intact. Here’s the updated, practical guide for Season 2.

### Why These Are Best in 12.1
Flasks – Unchanged foundation. Secondary-stat matching still gives the highest consistent value.
Food – The real winner of the patch. Loa’s Gathering and Feast of Knowledge deliver both stamina (survivability) and a high amount of your best secondary. They make the old primary-stat feasts (Midnight Masquerade / Divine Day) obsolete for endgame. Personal foods that target your highest secondary (especially Hearty versions) are more flexible than fixed-stat options and pair perfectly with modern gearing.
Health Potions – Concentrated Silvermoon is a pure numbers upgrade. Use it.
Combat Potions – Liquid Luster offers clean, drawback-free value with delayed peak power that often aligns better with real combat (especially after tank gathering in keys). It competes strongly with older options depending on your spec’s secondary weighting and ramp profile. Sim it.
