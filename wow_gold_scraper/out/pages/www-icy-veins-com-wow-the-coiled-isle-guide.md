# The Coiled Isle Zone Guide
Support Our Writers
## World of Warcraft: Midnight Zone Achievement Guides
The Coiled Isle is the new zone in Patch 12.1, housing the Vaults of Atal'Utek and the Zul'jarra's Forces renown. Learn how to reach the Isle and what can 
you do there!
## Unlocking the Coiled Isle
Access to the Coiled Isle is initially locked - trying to approach the isle will get you kicked 
out of the zone. To gain access to the Isle, you must start the Patch 12.1 storyline, which begins 
with the quest Hagar's Invitation in Silvermoon City.
Full access is unlocked upon completion of What Lies Beyond the Fog and allows you
to enter the Coiled Isle with your entire Warband.
## Coiled Isle Zone Achievement
Ula'tek Uncoiled is a meta achievement that requires you to complete several achievements 
in the Coiled Isle. Read on to learn how to complete all achievements involved,
and their rewards.
### Recommended Addons
We recommend installing TomTom , the 
premier WoW navigation assistant addon. TomTom is a lightweight addon that provides you with 
customizable waypoints and coordinates.
Throughout the guide, you will see multiple lines of text featuring coordinates. These can be 
input in TomTom on a separate text box via the /ttpaste command.
### Coiled Isle Achievement Rewards
Here are all the rewards you will earn while working on achievements in the Coiled Isle.
- Coiled to Strike gives you the Amani Forge housing decor.
- Although not explicitly a reward for Coiled to Strike , Coiled Isle rares have a chance to
  drop the Ruby Writhe and Topaz Skyfang mounts.
- Treasures of the Coiled Isle gives you the Auriferous Venomfang mount.
- Student of Hissstory gives you the Cursed Badge of the Soulcoilers toy.
- Tour of Duty: The Coiled Isle gives you the Ula'tek's Sssacrificial Rain toy.
- Angler of The Coiled Isle gives you the Dustlord title.
- Angler of The Coiled Isle gives you the Sinful Pearl customization.
- Cursebreaker gives you the Cursebreaker title.
## Activities in the Coiled Isle
The Coiled Isle gives players plenty of things to do. Among them you will find a new
world event to engage with, and many new treasures to unearth.
### Curse Surges
Curse Surges are a new world activity in the Coiled Isle. These are quick scenario events that 
spawn in 5 locations in the Coiled Isle, represented by a poison vignette on your map.
Curse Surges spawn roughly every hour and are comprised of an initial event where you need to 
clear objectives, and a final boss at the end. Completing a Curse Surge gives you a Venom-Soaked Satchel ,
which rewards Voidlight Marl and Adventurer Mistcrest .
### Coiled Isle Rare Mobs
The achievement Coiled to Strike requires you to kill every rare mob in the Coiled Isle. 
There are 12 rares in total for this achievement.
Each rare in the Coiled Isle grants 50 Reputation with the Zul'jarra's Forces renown faction on their first kill, and most of them are fairly straightforward. You can
also farm rares daily for Coffer Key Shard and Adventurer-track gear.

### Coiled Isle Lore Objects
The achievement Student of Hissstory requires you to locate all lore objects across the 
Coiled Isles. There are 10 lore objects, and they show up on your minimap with an hourglass vignette
Each lore object in the Coiled Isle grants 250 Reputation with the Zul'jarra's Forces renown faction, so completing this quick achievement is 
a nice way to get a head start on your renown grind!
Lore Object | Notes
Head Mason's Tablet | Inside the Gate of the Eastern Fang, on the corridor leading into the Vaults of Atal'Utek.
Amani Exile's Words | On the Western portion of Gnarldor Isle, under a fallen, hollowed out tree trunk leading
	into the ocean.
Worn Tablet | In the Whispering Marsh area, sitting in the inner part of the Altar of Wrath.
Abandoned Tablet | Just East of the Gate of the Eastern Fang, by the river where the rare Siltmouth is 
	sitting in.
Message in a Bottle | By the wreck of the Sethraliss's Scale boat South of the Forum, next to the Possessed Vase 
	treasure.
Oily Black Stone | By a pool of venom in the middle of the Venom Rise. The area is surrounded by Elite mobs, 
	but you can read the lore object without dismounting.
Forum Rules | On the Forum area, just Southwest of the Looming Mutagenitor Curse Surge.
Survival Journals | By an island Northeast of the Forum.
Zandalari Logbook | In the Eastern portion of Mlurkkr Mire, by the wreck of Paku's Talon.
Profaned Plaque | Inside the Gate of the Western Fang, inside a room just to the left when you enter the gate.
### Coiled Isle Side Quests
Like other zones, the Coiled Isle offers plenty of side quests for players to complete. Completing 
them awards reputation with the Zul'jarra's Forces renown faction, as well
as other cosmetic rewards.
- Completing a side quest in the Coiled Isle awards 50 Reputation with Zul'jarra's Forces.
- Completing the final quest of a side quest storyline in the Coiled Isle awards 250 
  Reputation with Zul'jarra's Forces.
