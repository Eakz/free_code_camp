# How to Farm Gold Fast in WoW Midnight (2026 Guide) | Mythic Store

Gold is more important than ever in World of Warcraft: Midnight . Between crafting, consumables, repairs, and Auction House prices, your gold disappears faster than your HP in a bad pull.
The good news? There are multiple efficient ways to farm gold in Midnight — but not all of them are worth your time.
This guide breaks down the fastest gold farming methods in 2026 , including solo farms, daily activities, and high-efficiency strategies used by experienced players.
# Gold Farming in Midnight — Quick Summary
If you just want the fastest methods:
- Delves (Tier 8+) – best consistent gold + rewards
- Prey Hunts – fast currency and vendor value
- Professions (Crafting + Gathering) – long-term gold
- Auction House flipping – highest potential profit
- World Quests & Events – steady daily income
If you combine 2–3 of these methods, you can easily maintain a strong gold flow.
# Best Gold Farming Methods in Midnight
## 1. Delves (Best Overall Gold Farm)
Delves are currently one of the most efficient gold sources in Midnight.
### Why Delves Are So Good
- Fast runs (10–15 minutes)
- Valuable loot and vendor items
- Currency rewards
- Great Vault progress (indirect value)
At Tier 8+ , rewards become significantly more valuable, including gear and materials that can be sold or used.
### How to Farm Efficiently
- Focus on Bountiful Delves
- Use keys on higher tiers only
- Optimize your route for fast clears
- Run multiple characters if possible
Delves are the best mix of gold + progression , which is why most players prioritize them.
## 2. Prey Hunts (Fast & Repeatable Gold)
Prey Hunts are one of the fastest open-world activities in Midnight.
### Why Prey Hunts Are Good
- Can be completed in 5–10 minutes
- Reward currency and loot
- Minimal setup required
- Great for casual farming sessions
Using the trap method (disarming traps instead of fighting), you can complete hunts extremely quickly.
### Best Strategy
- Focus only on disarming traps
- Ignore unnecessary combat
- Use Disarmed Trap consumables
- Chain multiple hunts back-to-back
Prey Hunts are perfect if you want quick gold without long sessions .
## 3. Professions (Best Long-Term Gold)
Professions are still one of the most reliable ways to make gold.
### Best Professions in Midnight
- Gathering (Herbalism, Mining)
- Crafting (Enchanting, Alchemy, Blacksmithing)
### Why Professions Work
- Constant demand for materials
- Consumables always sell
- Crafting gear and enchants = high value
### Pro Tip
Early in the season, materials and crafted items are extremely expensive. This is when professions make the most gold.
If you want stable long-term income , professions are mandatory.
## 4. Auction House Flipping (High Skill, High Reward)
If you understand the market, this is the fastest way to make large amounts of gold .
### How It Works
- Buy items at a low price
- Sell them at a higher price
- Repeat at scale
### What to Flip
- Crafting materials
- Consumables
- Rare BoE gear
- Event items
### Why It's Powerful
- No grinding required
- Scales with your gold
- Can generate massive profit
This method requires experience but offers the highest gold potential .
## 5. World Quests & Weekly Activities
World content provides steady, reliable gold income.
### Good Sources
- World Quests
- Weekly events
- World bosses
- Assaults
### Pros
- Easy and accessible
- No setup required
- Good for casual players
### Cons
- Daily/weekly limits
- Lower gold per hour
Best used as a supplement , not your main farming method.
# Advanced Gold Farming Tips
## Combine Methods
The best players don't rely on one method.
Example routine:
- Do daily Delves
- Run a few Prey Hunts
- Sell materials or items on AH
This creates multiple income streams .
## Don't Waste Time on Low-Value Farms
If something feels slow, it probably is.
Focus on:
- Fast activities
- High-value rewards
- Repeatable content
## Track Market Trends
Prices change constantly in World of Warcraft .
- Sell when prices are high
- Buy when prices are low
- Watch patch changes
## Use Multiple Characters
More characters = more:
- Delves
- Prey Hunts
- Weekly rewards
This significantly increases your total gold income.
# What About "Cheap Gold"?
You might see offers for extremely cheap gold online. In many cases, this gold comes from automated farming (bots), which can lead to:
- Account risks
- Unstable markets
- Low-quality delivery
If you care about your account safety, it's better to stick with reliable sources .
# Final Thoughts
Gold farming in Midnight is all about efficiency.
### Best Methods Recap
- Best overall: Delves
- Fastest farming: Prey Hunts
- Best long-term: Professions
- Highest potential: Auction House
If you combine these methods, you'll always have enough gold for:
- Gear upgrades
- Consumables
- Crafting
- Endgame progression
