## What Is a Bountiful Delve?
A Bountiful Delve is an enhanced version of a regular Delve.
It uses the same basic location, story structure, enemies, boss, and difficulty system, but adds a special reward chest at the end.
Bountiful Delves are identified through their highlighted map icons and entrance information. The entrance panel shows that a Bountiful Coffer is available and that a Restored Coffer Key is required to unlock it.
The Delve itself can still be completed without spending a key. However, the main reason to prioritize a Bountiful Delve is the stronger equipment contained inside its locked Coffer.

## How Bountiful Coffers Work
A Bountiful Coffer appears in the final treasure room after successfully completing an active Bountiful Delve.
The Coffer requires one Restored Coffer Key.
Opening it provides a piece of equipment based on the completed Delve tier, along with other possible seasonal rewards.
#### Do You Need a Key to Enter?
No.
A Restored Coffer Key is not required to enter or finish the Delve.
The key is only needed to open the locked Bountiful Coffer after defeating the final boss.
This allows you to complete the adventure even when you do not have a key, although you will miss its primary gearing reward.
#### Does the Key Improve the Entire Delve?
No.
The key unlocks the special chest. It does not directly increase enemy difficulty, boss strength, or the quality of every standard treasure found during the run.
The completed tier determines the Coffer reward.
## What Is a Restored Coffer Key?
A Restored Coffer Key is the currency required to open a Bountiful Coffer.
Keys can be obtained directly or restored by collecting enough Coffer Key Shards from eligible Season 2 outdoor activities and rewards. The system automatically converts the required number of shards into a usable key.
Restored Coffer Keys can be stored until you are ready to use them.
This makes key management an important part of efficient Delve gearing.

#### Should You Spend Every Key Immediately?
Not always.

If your current Delve tier only provides gear below what you already wear, saving the key for a stronger tier may produce more value.
However, do not save every key indefinitely when an immediate upgrade would help you clear harder content.

## WoW Midnight Bountiful Delves FAQ
#### What is a Bountiful Delve?
A Bountiful Delve is a specially marked Delve containing a locked Bountiful Coffer with stronger gear.
#### What opens a Bountiful Coffer?
One Restored Coffer Key.
#### Do you need a key to enter a Bountiful Delve?
No. The key is only needed to open the special Coffer.
#### How do you get Restored Coffer Keys?
Keys can be obtained directly or restored from Coffer Key Shards earned through eligible Season 2 activities.
#### What is the best Bountiful Delve tier for gearing?
Tier 8 is the main general gearing target because it provides Champion-track Coffer gear and supports Hero-track Trovehunter and Great Vault rewards.
#### What does a Tier 8 Bountiful Coffer give?
Tier 8 and higher Bountiful Coffers can provide Champion 2/6 gear.
#### Can Tier 8 Delves give Hero gear?
Yes. Trovehunter’s Bounty and the Great Vault can provide Hero 1/6 equipment from Tier 8 and higher progression.
#### Does Tier 11 improve the standard Great Vault reward?
No. Tier 8 already reaches the standard Hero 1/6 World-row ceiling.
#### Why should I run Tier 11?
Tier 11 supports high-end Mistcrest progression, seasonal achievements, Delver’s Journey rewards, and Ascendant Venomstones.
#### What is Trovehunter’s Bounty?
It is a special Delve map that adds a Hidden Trove containing stronger gear to an eligible completion.
#### What tier should I use Trovehunter’s Bounty on?
Use it on the highest tier you can complete consistently. Tier 8 and above can provide Hero 1/6 equipment.
#### Can Bountiful Delves give tier-set gear?
Bountiful Coffers provide eligible World gear that may be converted through the Catalyst. World-row Great Vault choices can also directly contain a class-set piece.
#### Do Catalyst conversions keep Delve armor stats?
Yes. Season 2 Catalyst items preserve the original armor’s secondary statistics.
#### What are Afflicted Souls?
They are Season 2 items that can drop from Heavy Trunks in Tier 6 and higher Bountiful Delves and can improve Nightmare Hunt progression and rewards.
#### Who is the Delve companion in Midnight?
Valeera Sanguinar.
#### Can Valeera tank or heal?
Yes. Valeera can be assigned as a tank, healer, or damage dealer and customized with Curios.
#### Can Bountiful Delves be completed in a group?
Yes. Delves support solo play and groups of up to five players.
#### Are Bountiful Delves timed?
No. You can move carefully, wait for cooldowns, and use controlled pulls.
