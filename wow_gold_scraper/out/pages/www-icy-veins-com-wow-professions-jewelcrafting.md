# Jewelcrafting Guide for Midnight
Support Our Writers
This page covers the most important aspects of Jewelcrafting in Midnight, 
including talent builds and suggestions on how to make gold with the profession.
## Jewelcrafting in Midnight
Jewelcrafting makes rings, including Embellished items like Loa Worshiper's Band , 
amulets, such as the Thalassian Phoenix Torque , reagents like Sunglass Vial , 
profession accessories, such as Attuned Thalassian Rune-Prism , gems such 
as Flawless Versatile Garnet , and House Decor.
They also Prospect Mining ores for most of their uncut gems, 
which also provides Duskshrouded Stone and Crystalline Glass reagents 
as common byproducts.
Finally, they can Crush uncut gems to obtain Crushed Gemstones , 
which are an essential component of many Jewelcrafting crafts.
### Jewelcrafting Changes in Season 2
Jewelcrafting can now learn the new Polished Ammolite Embellishment craft, 
and some new Decor pieces with the new Design: Roaring Amani Garnet , Design: Piercing Amani Lapis , 
and Design: Opalescent Amani Peridot recipes.
There are also new skill 50 trainer recipes which allow you to convert 10 rank 
1 Crystalline Glass or 18 Duskshrouded Stone into their rank 2 versions. 
As these are mostly used for making Rank 2 Sunglass Vial , Rank 2 vial price 
and Alchemy potion prices are likely to come down as a result.
## Best Race for Jewelcrafting in Midnight
There are no Horde races with Jewelcrafting bonuses, but Alliance players can be Draenei for +5 skill points or Kul'Tiran for +2 skill points.

Jewelcrafting can make great use of all four stats, but you might want to focus on 
specific stats with different Jewelcrafting specializations. We go into detail with

## Leveling Jewelcrafting in Midnight
Increasing your skill is done by crafting items that grant skill points as usual, 
and in order to be efficient, we recommend trying to get as many first-craft bonuses 
while leveling as possible, which also grant experience if you are not at 
maximum level.
Besides learning basic recipes from the trainer, you can also learn extra recipes 
beyond the profession trainer by buying them with Moxie from reputation vendors 
and the vendor next to the profession table in Silvermoon. These will grant more 
first crafts and can occasionally be the most efficient way to level in the later 
levels.
## How to Level Jewelcrafting in Midnight
### Leveling Jewelcrafting to 65 Skill
As with most professions, getting to skill 65 can easily be done through trainer 
recipes, with the remaining skill points being awarded by specialty crafts such 
as Indecipherable Eversong Diamond , Masterwork Sin'dorei Band , and Attuned Thalassian Rune-Prism which you should do based on your choice of 
profession talents.
In order to quickly level up uninterrupted, ensure you have the following materials:
- 55 of the cheapest Midnight ore available
- 15 Refulgent Copper Ore
- 15 Umbral Tin Ore
- 73 Crystalline Glass
- 9 Duskshrouded Stone
- 3 Glimmering Gemdust
- 3 Harandar Peridot
Start by prospecting the cheap 55 Midnight ore to get to skill 12, and afterwards 
craft:
- 3 Sunglass Vial
- 3 Quick Peridot
- 3 Gleaming Copper Band
- 3 Nocturnal Charm
- 29 Monologuer's Chalice (on average)
While you can start crafting end game recipes earlier instead of relying on the 
Jewelcrafting trainer, these will generally be more efficient to craft towards the 
end of your leveling once regular trainer recipes stop giving reliable skill points, 
as they are expensive to produce and will generally only turn a profit when crafted 
at maximum rank with Concentration .

you invest into with your Jewelcrafting Knowledge. Since skill-ups are more RNG as 
you get closer to 100 Jewelcrafting Skill, we recommend checking the Auction House 
or using one of our recommended 
crafting Addons to help you skill up without it eating into your wallet too much.
Thankfully the process isn't too difficult, as you can craft the same type of

## Jewelcrafting Knowledge in Midnight
You will be able to track all weekly and one-time sources of profession knowledge 
with Myu's Knowledge Points Tracker , 
which has been converted to an addon in Midnight!
There are also eight treasures around the world that you can loot to gain up 
to 24 extra knowledge. You can find them all easily in-game with the aforementioned 
addon.
## Best Jewelcrafting Specializations and Talents in Midnight
One of your top gold making options is gem cutting with a heavy investment into 
the Glamorous Gems tree and its many sub-nodes. Green gems primarily 
give Haste , Purple gems primarily give Mastery , Red gems primarily give Crit , and Blue gems primarily give Versatility , 
allowing you to fine tune which gem stats to focus on while navigating through the 
tree.
Gems are good targets for Concentration builds, especially now that you 
can get a 100% refund when Inspiration procs through the Thoughtful Throughput tree and its Calculated Concentration sub-node. The other nodes on this 
tree grant massive stat bonuses and improvements to your general efficiency as well.
Alternatively, the Alluring Accessories tree focuses on gear crafting, 
with sub-nodes focused on amulets, rings, and profession accessories.
Finally, to improve your skill and efficiency at Prospecting , Crushing , 
and crafting (optional) reagents, invest into the Proficient Processor tree 
and its sub-nodes.
## Making Gold with Jewelcrafting in Midnight
Ideally, Jewelcrafting allows you to convert cheap ores into expensive gems and 
other reagents, or endgame gear. Due to the commodity nature of reagents and gems, 
it is very likely that the market will quickly get loaded with crafts that are only 
marginally more expensive than the materials used to create them.
Until you fully max out skill, gear, and knowledge for your desired crafts, you 
will be reliant on Concentration to get maximum ranks out, which can quickly 
be exhausted every day, so make sure to create some alts to increase your production 
cap while it is still very profitable to do so! Even later on those can still be 
useful as they will be able to create maximum rank items more cheaply than the 
guaranteed method, which requires full maximum Rank reagents.
Gear crafting is expected to hold out for longer, but the epic crafts will 
require you to find customers willing to tip well.
