## World of Warcraft: Midnight Delve Guides
Delves in Midnight offer a blend of quick, challenging adventures that are perfect for both solo 
players and groups. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know, from basic 
mechanics and strategic tips to the rewards you can earn.

With the launch of Patch 12.1, a few new things are coming to Delves:
- Three new Delves in The Coiled Isle : Gnarldor Isle , Ring of Glory , and Venomfall Deeps (Nemesis Delve).
- New Ula'tek-themed storylines for several Midnight Delves: Collegiate Calamity , Parhelion Plaza , Shadow Enclave , Atal'Aman , Twilight Crypts , Grudge Pit , and Shadowguard Point .
- New Delver's Journey for Midnight Season 2 and Amani-themed 
 wares at the Delver's Headquarters .

want her to use.
## What Are Delves?
Delves are short, 10-15 minute scenarios designed to fit into your gameplay schedule and 
contribute to your character's progression, as they provide credit toward the Great Vault rewards in Midnight.
Unlike dungeons and raids, Delves can be completed solo or with a group of up to 5 players. The 
flexibility combined with their role-agnostic nature makes Delves accessible to all types of players.
### Key Features
Delves are designed to be replayable, with varying levels of difficulty and rewards. The season 
companion can be customized to fit your playstyle, whether you need a healer, a damage dealer, or a 
tank character. Delves have 11 Tiers of difficulty, providing a challenging experience for players 
of all skill levels.
### Navigating Delves: Finding Entrances on the Map
When exploring Quel'Thalas, you will find each delve marked on your Map with a unique cave icon, making it easier to locate them as you travel through the zones.
As you approach the icon on the Map, check your surroundings for cave openings, ruins, and other 
visual cues in the environment.

### Understanding the Delves UI Window at the Entrance
When you approach the entrance to a Delve, you will be greeted by a special User Interface window
that provides important information to prepare you for your run.
At the top of the UI, the name of the Delve is displayed. Beneath it, you will find a dropdown 
menu that lets you select the Tier of difficulty. Higher tiers are more challenging, but also give 
you better rewards.
The UI also provides a brief description of your main objectives in a Delve. For example, your
task is to disrupt the Shadowguard Ethereal operations. Make sure to read this before entering, so 
you are aware of the primary goals.
On the right side, the UI lists the rewards you will receive upon completing the Delve. Rewards 
typically include Experience for your companion and the treasure room contains chests that you can 
open for gear, Voidlight Marl , Midnight profession reagents, and more.
## Meet Valeera Sanguinaar, the Midnight Delve Companion
Valeera Sanguinar will be your Delve companion throughout the Midnight expansion.
Your NPC companion comes with a fully customizable role and curios, allowing you to tailor her 
abilities to best support your journey through the various Delves in Quel'Thalas.
To make the most of Valeera's abilities, check out our comprehensive guide to Valeera Sanguinar, 
where we dive deep into her Talent Tree, best curios to equip, and strategies for optimizing her 
performance in your Delve adventures.
## Bountiful Delves
Bountiful Delves have glowing icons compared to regular Delves and offer increased rewards 
because they provide access to Bountiful Coffers found at the end of your run. These special 
chests can be opened with Restored Coffer Key s that you can get by collecting Coffer Key Shard s from world activities and quests.
A timer will be displayed indicating the remaining time before another Delve becomes Bountiful.
As a rule, Bountiful Delves are rerolled on the daily reset.
You should always prioritize completing Bountiful Delves over regular Delves to get better loot.
Non-Bountiful Delves do not have scaling rewards past Tier 3.
## Gearing in Delves: Earn Progress Towards the Great Vault
Regular Delves only drop non-upgradeable gear regardless of the tier, which is why you should focus 
on completing Bountiful Delves that offer better gear with increasing difficulty.
Completing 2/4/8 Delves unlocks items in the Great Vault the following week! To maximize
your rewards and receive Hero-track gear in your vault, you should complete 8 Tier 8 runs every week.
Check out our separate guide for even more information about how the Great Vault, commonly referred to
as the Weekly Chest, works.
It is important to remember that, for End of Delve runs, loot rewards are capped out at Tier 
3. For Tier 4+ Delves, it is recommended to only do them while they're Bountiful and you have Restored Coffer Key s available to use.

### Delves Difficulty
Delves are divided into 11 tiers of difficulty, with Tier 1 being the easiest and Tier 11 the 
most difficult.
To unlock Tier 4+, you need to earn the Midnight Delves: Endgame achievement on at least 1 
character.
You do not need to unlock tiers for each delve separately. When you complete, say, Sunkiller Sanctum on Tier 7, Tier 8 will unlock for all Delves.
It is important to note that loot scales only up to Tier 8. Tiers 9-11 are for crests only and do
 not offer better loot.

### Delver's Bounty
Once per week, you can loot Delver's Bounty from Delves, with the biggest chance coming 
from Azta'rec , the Delve nemesis for Midnight Season 2. Bringing him down to 50% 
will spawn a pile of gold containing a Bounty and other loot. Using a Delver's Bounty map during the same 
run will provide you with a Hidden Trove at the end of your run, scaling with the 
tier of your Delve.
Scalebound Herald's Flute is an item you can buy from Naleidea Rivergleam at the Delver's
Headquarters in Silvermoon for 5,000 Undercoin s. With this item, you can summon Az'tarec to your delve after reaching the checkpoint. Here is how to do it:
- Enter a delve and reach the delve checkpoint, clear the surrounding area.
- Then, use Scalebound Herald's Flute to summon Az'tarec and bring him down to 50%.
- Loot the gold pile, get your Delver's Bounty , and use it before killing the boss of the Delve you are already in .
## Delve Mechanics in Midnight Season 2
Delves feature various mechanics that improve the depth and complexity of each run. Below are key
elements you will encounter during your runs.
### Nemesis Strongbox
At Tier 4+, Azta'rec's allies will be present in Delves in the form of Ula'tek's Chosen. Watch 
out for them, as they are Elite mobs that are more powerful compared then the regular mobs you will 
face during your runs.
Defeating these packs will provide you with better loot inside the Nemesis Strongbox at the
end of your run. A message will pop up when your loot in that chest is upgraded.
You can see the location of all Nemesis packs on your map. They will be indicated with gray
helm icons .
Nemesis Strongboxes mostly contain crafting materials.
### Mislaid Curiosities, Boons, and Blessings
Early on in your Midnight Delver's Journey you will unlock boons and blessings. These are
temporary buffs to make Delve completion easier.
Mislaid Curiosities are small bags found on the ground that contain companion experience,
Boons, and Delve consumables. These can also sometimes contain Midnight profession reagents, and
can also be used to activate certain curio abilities for your Delve companion.
Boons are obtained from looting Mislaid Curiosities throughout Delves and give you 
temporary stat buffs for the Delve you're currently in:
- Boon of Abstinence : Increases your lowest secondary stat.
- Boon of Fortitude : Reduces damage taken.
- Boon of Possibilities : Increases a random secondary stat.
- Boon of Potency : Increases your highest secondary stat.
- Boon of Power : Increases your primary stat.
- Boon of Vigor : Increases your maximum health and movement speed.
Blessings are obtained from looting the chests at the end of Delves, and grant you a buff that
lasts either 24 hours, or until you complete your next Delve. These buffs only apply inside Delves.
- Blessing of Zeal : Your harmful spells and abilities have a chance to cause an explosion
on impact, inflicting moderate Fire damage split evenly between nearby enemies.
- Blessing of Potency : All stats increased by 10%.
- Crimson Blessing : You are granted one additional Delve life.
- Sentinel's Blessing : Gain 50% of your maximum health as an absorption shield for 1 minute
whenever your health falls below 20% during combat. Can only occur once every 5 minutes.
- Veiled Blessing : Knock back surrounding enemies when you get hit in melee range. Can 
only occur once every minute.
### Gilded Crests from Tier 11 Bountiful Delves
Completing a Tier 11 Bountiful delve with lives remaining will grant you a Gilded Stash ,
which gives you 7 Myth Mistcrest s. You can complete this up to 3 times for a total of 21 
Myth Mistcrests from Delves per week. You do not need Restored Coffer Key s to open them.
This is unlocked at Rank 4 of the Midnight Season 2 Delver's Journey.
## Delve Basics
Here are some basics that apply to all delve runs.
### Explorers' League Supplies
At the beginning of each delve and at its checkpoint, you will find Explorers' League 
Supplies . This interactive object allows you to repair your gear or set up curios and
specializations for your NPC Companion.
### Delve Objectives
Most Delves consist of 2-3 scenario-like objectives that must be completed before you can face 
the final boss. These objectives will change every day, on the daily reset. There are multiple 
variations of objectives for each Delve.
As an example, in one of the Grudge Pit variants, you need to enter an arena tournament by 
facing different waves of Fungalings as you participate in their championship. Another variant
has you helping Fungalings against a Lightbloom Rutaani invasion.
### Sturdy Chests
Inside Delves, you will come across Sturdy Chests hidden in various locations. These are a
one-time chest loot that contain companion experience and Voidlight Marl .
Looting all Sturdy Chests inside all Midnight Delves grants the Midnight: Leave No Treasure Unfound achievement, which grants the title Seeker of Loot and is part of the Glory of the Midnight Delver meta achievement.
### Rare Elites and Special Powers
Inside Delves, Rare Elites will be marked on your map with a special icon. Once defeated, they 
will grant you or Valeera a powerful buff for the rest of your run.
In Midnight Season 2, you can obtain 4 possible powers from Delve Rare Elites. This time around, 
Special Powers are not attached to rares. Instead, which one you get is random, and there can be more than one 
Rare Elite in a Delve at a time. Here is a list of possible powers for the Season:
- Poisoned Pace : Increases movement speed by 25%. Increases further for each Poisoned 
creature within 20 yards.
- Poisonous Platelets : Poisons your blood, causing creatures to attack you to occasionally 
take Nature damage.
- Stomach Turner : Occasionally poison creatures in combat, causing them to take Nature 
damage over time while vomiting out slime.
- Unstable Venom Sacs : Occasionally generate venom sacs while in combat. Walking over a 
venom sac squirts a wave out of it, dealing Nature damage to enemies in its path.
### Checkpoints in Delves
As you progress through Delves, you will find strategically-placed checkpoints close to the final
objective before the boss encounter. These checkpoints look like summoning stones.
After you reach a checkpoint, when you die, you will respawn at the checkpoint, instead of the 
start of the Delve. At these checkpoints you can sell gear, adjust Valeera 's abilities, 
and repair your gear. Reaching the checkpoint will also grant you an additional Revive.
### Revives in Delves
When you enter a delve, you will notice a "Revives Remaining" counter in the Delve UI. The 
counter shows how many times you and your party members can die and respawn inside the same Delve. 
Every time a player releases their spirit after death, one revive is subtracted from the total.
The number of revives remaining at the end of the Delve directly impacts the quality of the 
rewards you receive at the end of your run, so it is very important to play cautiously. As you
progress in Delve tiers, you will start out with fewer revives:
- Tiers 1-3 : No death limit.
- Tiers 4-8 : 5 Revives.
- Tiers 9: 4 Revives.
- Tiers 10-11: 3 Revives.
When you lose all your revives in a Bountiful Delve, you will not receive a Bountiful Chest at the end of it. If this happens to you, it is recommended to zone out and start anew.
### Final Boss
At the end of the Delve you must fight a boss to unlock the treasure room and reap your rewards. The 
final boss might change between Delve variants, and some Delve variants may not have a final boss at
all, but a final objective that does reflect a similar difficulty to a final boss.
For each death while facing the final boss, you will receive a stack of Delver's Determination . It increases your health, damage dealt, and healing received by 5% 
per stack.
### Treasure Room
When you defeat the final boss, you will reach the Treasure Room, which consists of multiple loot
chests. These chests usually are composed of two Delve Trunks, one Bountiful Coffer (if you've 
completed a Bountiful Delve), a Nemesis Supplies chest (if you've defeated any Nemesis packs in The
Delve), and a Gilded Stash (if completing a Delve on Tier 11 after unlocking it in the Delver's Journey ).
### Bountiful Coffer
Found only inside Bountiful Delves, the Bountiful Coffer is an epic chest that contains the best possible
loot. It requires a Restored Coffer Key to open.
## Delver's Journey in Midnight Season 2
Completing Bountiful Delves in Midnight Season 2 grants credit 
toward a progression system called Delver's Journey . Upon reaching certain milestones, you 
will unlock additional rewards for Delves such as improvements and cosmetic rewards.
Check out our Delver's Journey guide for more information about this season's rewards!
## Delver's Headquarters, Your Base of Operations in Midnight
The Delver's Headquarter is located in central Silvermoon City. Valeera Sanguinar has a base 
set up here, which serves as the main Delves hub and vendor location in the expansion.
To learn more about the Delver's Headquarters and the vendors you will find there, check out our 
dedicated guide linked below.
## Weekly Delves Quest
Every once in a while, you will receive a quest from Valeera Sanguinar at the Delver's 
Headquarters that offers 1 Pinnacle Cache , a weekly cache containing materials for 
upgrading, gear, gold, and more. To earn the cache, you must complete 3 Delves. Keep in mind that this
quest is on a rotation with other weekly quests, and will not be available every week.
## Delves Bonus Event
Every once in a while, a world event will be active that is directly tied to Delves! For the whole week
the Sign of the Explorer buff will grant you a +25% increase to Delver's Journey gains, 25% Valeera XP gains, and 25% Undercoin gains.
The weekly quest offered by Archmage Aethas Sunreaver in Silvermoon is called A Call to Delves and requires you to complete 5 Delves for 1 piece of Item Level 246 Champion 
1/6 gear.
## Delve Rewards in Midnight
Rewards for Delves in Midnight Season 2 include many cosmetics like Sin'dorei Golem mounts and 
more. Check out our separate guide to learn more about the seasonal rewards that await!
