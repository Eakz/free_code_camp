# WoW Gold Farming Routes: Top Gold-per-Hour Methods in Midnight · FullBoosts

If you've Googled "wow gold farming routes" and clicked through ten posts that all say "do your dailies," you're owed a real guide. This one only lists methods with actual gold-per-hour numbers, sourced from current TSM market data on US-Stormrage and EU-Kazzak.
Skip to the method that fits your time budget. None of these are bot routes; all are hand-played and TOS-clean.
## How to read the gold-per-hour numbers
The numbers below are average over a 4-hour session at a specific TSM market state. Your numbers will vary by:
- Realm population. High-pop realms move volume faster and price competitively; low-pop realms have higher per-item profit but lower volume.
- Patch timing. First two weeks of a patch see 2-3x average gold/hr on most farming methods because demand spikes.
- Time of day. AH activity peaks during US/EU prime hours. Off-peak farming has 30-50% lower gold/hr because fewer buyers are listing competitive prices.
The numbers below are realistic averages. Top earners outperform by 50-100% via market intuition; new gold farmers underperform by 30-50% in their first week.
## Tier 1 - High gold per hour (200K+ /hr)
These methods require capital, market knowledge, or a high-end character. They're the highest paying but have the steepest learning curve.
### Auction house flipping (200K-500K /hr at scale)
Buy underpriced commodities (raw materials, BoE epics, profession recipes) and relist at market price. Requires:
- 1-2M gold of working capital to start.
- TradeSkillMaster (TSM) addon configured with current realm prices.
- 30-60 minutes daily of disciplined posting/canceling.
The skill is recognizing what's "underpriced" - which depends on tracking the realm's average for 2 weeks before you start flipping. Skip flipping for the first month of a patch; volatility punishes new flippers.
### High-tier profession crafting (150K-300K /hr if you have BiS recipes)
The Spelltome reagents introduced in Midnight have a tight production margin (5-10% per craft) but high volume. With BiS Inscription gear and the right recipes, you can craft Spelltomes 8 hours a day at a 30-50K profit per stack of 10. Requires the Spelltome recipe (raid drop) and Inscription at max with full Hero Talent investment.
Engineering tinkers and Alchemy specialty potions are the other two top crafting routes; both require 100K+ in mats per session and a profession at 80+.
### Mythic+ keystone selling (250K-400K per +20 timed run, ~3 runs/hr ceiling)
If you're a 3500+ rated M+ player, you can sell carries to other players at 300-500K per timed +20 key, paid in gold. This is technically a separate gold-making category from "farming" but is one of the highest paying activities in the game.
We facilitate the buyer side at WoW Mythic+ services - if you're a top-percentile M+ player and want to monetize, the supply side is open.
## Tier 2 - Mid gold per hour (50K-150K /hr)
These methods balance accessibility with reasonable returns. Most players spend their farming time here.
### Old-raid solo runs (80K-200K /hr depending on era)
Cataclysm and Mists of Pandaria raids on Heroic, soloed at level cap. Drops are mostly transmog-tier but the gold/hr from raw vendor + occasional rare BoE is solid. A maxed-out routine:
- Mondays: BoE-heavy raids (Firelands, Throne of Thunder, Siege of Orgrimmar)
- Wednesdays: Cataclysm transmog runs (Dragon Soul Heroic, Bastion of Twilight)
- Fridays: Mists progression (Throne of Thunder for sigils, Siege for cosmetics)
Best done on a melee class with self-heals (Ret Pally, Frost DK, BM Hunter). Tank specs are slower per pull but more consistent.
### Profession dailies + work orders (60K-100K /hr passive)
Profession knowledge dailies plus accepting work orders (when your skill level is competitive). Set this up once and it runs in the background of whatever else you're doing. Inscription, Alchemy, and Engineering have the highest passive return; Tailoring and Leatherworking are mid; Skinning and Mining work but are bounded by node availability.
### Raw material farming during patch week (100K-180K /hr in week 1, drops to 50K /hr by week 4)
Herbalism and Mining nodes in current zones are inflated for the first 2-3 weeks of every patch because crafters are stockpiling. Run a 30-min loop on a high-pop realm with herbalism + mining, sell on the AH. The first weeks pay well; by week 4, the market saturates and per-hour drops by half.
## Tier 3 - Lower gold per hour but stable (15K-50K /hr)
These methods don't pay great per hour but they're stable, low-skill, and pleasant to do.
### Daily quest hubs (15K-30K /hr)
Faction-rep quest hubs (Slayer's Duellum, Silvermoon Court, Hara'ti) reward gold + tokens that vendor. Plus the rep itself unlocks profession recipes, mounts, and transmog with significant gold value. The "boring" answer to gold farming and the most common method.
### Pet battle dailies + cage flipping (20K-40K /hr)
Pet battling dailies pay 8-15K each and unlock rare battle pets. Cage them and sell on AH for 2-50K per pet depending on rarity. Requires a leveled battle pet team but is one of the lowest-stress farms.
### Tailoring cloth runs in old zones (15K-25K /hr)
Specific old zones (Mount Hyjal, Tol Barad) have mob-density spots where 15-min runs net 50-100 cloth bolts. Sells modestly but stacks up. Best as a side activity, not a main farm.
## Methods to skip (low gold per hour or high risk)
- Currency exchange / token buying - converting WoW Tokens at retail prices is a worse return than ANY of the above per hour worked. Tokens are for converting fiat to playtime, not for gold farming.
- Pet battle PvP - takes hours, pays in pet charms not gold.
- Random world quest grinding - 5-10K /hr, the worst pay for the time spent.
- Buying gold from a botted source - not a gold-farming method, this is account suicide. Bot gold gets caught in mass-bans.
## Strategic gold-making vs. tactical gold-making
Two different time horizons:
- Tactical is "I need 500K by Saturday for my mount." Run Tier 2 farms for 4-6 hours, you'll hit it.
- Strategic is "I want my account to passively make 5M gold over the season." Set up profession knowledge + work orders + daily AH posts and let it compound.
Most players don't think strategic. Setting up the passive income is the highest-impact 4 hours you'll spend on gold-making in the entire expansion. Do it once, it pays for the next 6 months.
## When buying WoW gold makes more sense than farming
If your time is worth more than the per-hour rate of farming, buying gold is the rational choice. The math:
- Tier 1 farms (200K+ /hr): farming pays best if your time is worth less than ~$10/hr (current gold-to-USD conversion).
- Tier 2 farms (50-150K /hr): buying makes sense above ~$5/hr time value.
- Tier 3 farms (15-50K /hr): buying is the better choice for almost anyone with a job.
Real player time value at most US/EU income brackets exceeds Tier 3's per-hour rate; buying gold for casual goals is the rational outcome. We carry WoW Gold on every major US/EU realm with hand-delivered (no bot) listings, sorted by lowest price.
## Frequently asked questions
### What's the highest gold-per-hour method in WoW Midnight?
Mythic+ keystone selling (300K-500K per timed +20 run) tops the list, but it requires a 3500+ rated M+ character. AH flipping with TSM and 1-2M working capital is second (200-500K /hr). Profession crafting with BiS recipes is third (150-300K /hr).
### How much gold per hour can a new player realistically make?
A fresh level-cap character without professions or capital makes 15-30K /hr through dailies and basic farms. After a week of building rep tokens and a profession to 80, that doubles to 40-60K /hr. After a month with professions maxed and 200K of working capital, 80-120K /hr is realistic.
### Is buying WoW gold safer than farming?
Buying gold from a verified seller (hand-delivered, not bot-delivered) has a low ban-risk profile when the trade is a normal player-to-player transaction. Bot-delivered gold is dangerous because Blizzard's mass bans target the supplying network, which can flag the buyer. Browse verified WoW gold sellers for hand-delivered listings.
### What's the best class for gold farming?
Beast Mastery Hunter and Vengeance Demon Hunter are the two best soloers for old-raid runs. Outlaw Rogue is the best for skipping dungeon trash to speed-run rare drops. For AH flipping, your class doesn't matter at all - market intuition is the skill, not gameplay.
### How much gold do I actually need in Midnight?
Median gear-and-mat investment for a Mythic raid main: 500K-1M gold per patch. M+ pusher: 200-400K. Casual completionist with mount goals: 5M+ across the expansion (some mounts cost 1-5M each). Most players over-target their gold goals; figure out what you specifically need before farming for an arbitrary number.
